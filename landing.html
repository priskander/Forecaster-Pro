Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Forecaster Pro.

Can I use Forecaster Pro without an active internet connection? ↓

Yes! Forecaster Pro is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App. All transactions, grocery disbursements, medical logs, and fleet entries are committed to local persistent storage immediately and synchronize with the encrypted cloud ledger once your device reconnects.

How does the intelligent stock depletion calculation work? ↓

Forecaster Pro monitors item purchase dates, registered package volumes, and average consumption intervals. The system dynamically flags items when cumulative days remaining fall below your chosen threshold (e.g. <7 days) or when base pill count triggers a low-stock alert.

Can payment accounts be edited or deleted once created? ↓

In compliance with strict financial ledger audit standards, payment accounts are locked permanently once established to protect historic transfer and reconciliation integrity.

How do sub-users and roles work? ↓

Primary users can provision team or family members with dedicated roles (Manager, Member, Viewer). Each sub-user receives isolated credentials with mandatory initial password reset, and access can be revoked by the Primary user anytime.
